**QuantumFusion** is an exploratory quantum-classical digital twin prototype designed to study boundary magnetic field trimming and tearing mode mitigation in next-generation tokamaks (ITER / SPARC class). By formulating the continuous electromagnetic boundary condition as an all-to-all Ising spin-glass Hamiltonian, the system leverages **IonQ's native trapped-ion all-to-all connectivity model** to execute QAOA (Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm) circuits with **zero SWAP routing overhead** and a **$4.33\times$ reduction in circuit depth** compared to planar nearest-neighbor architectures.

## 1. Problem Statement: Tokamak Plasma Disruptions

In magnetic confinement fusion devices, sustaining a high-temperature plasma core ($> 10^8\text{ K}$) requires dynamic equilibrium against magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) instabilities. Resonant tearing modes ($m=2, n=1$ magnetic islands at rational safety factor $q=2$) cause magnetic field flutter, threatening thermal quench and rapid heat dumps onto divertor targets in $< 1\text{ ms}$ (with heat flux exceeding stationary engineering limits of $10\text{ MW/m}^2$).

External 3D Resonant Magnetic Perturbation (RMP) correction coils suppress islands by applying counter-phased helical magnetic fields. However, determining the optimal discrete coil polarity vector $s \in \{-1, +1\}^N$ against dynamic magnetic field drift across microsecond timescales is a non-convex combinatorial search problem over a discrete $2^N$ configuration space where classical combinatorial heuristics can stagnate in suboptimal local energy minima.

## 2. Theoretical Formulation & Ising Mapping

### 2.1 Continuous Biot-Savart Induction Kernel
The vacuum magnetic field generated by an array of $N=8$ external toroidal correction coils at any spatial boundary point $\mathbf{r}$ is governed by the Biot-Savart line integral:

$$\mathbf{B}_m(\mathbf{r}) = \frac{\mu_0 I_m}{4\pi} \oint_{C_m} \frac{d\mathbf{l} \times (\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}')}{|\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'|^3}$$

To mitigate island growth, the superposition of control fields must cancel the normal component of the perturbing tearing mode error field $\mathbf{B}_{\text{pert}}(\mathbf{r})$ over the rational surface $\mathcal{S}_{q=2}$:

$$\min_{s \in \{-1, +1\}^N} \mathcal{F}(s) = \int_{\mathcal{S}} \left| \sum_{m=1}^N s_m \mathbf{B}_m(\mathbf{r}) + \mathbf{B}_{\text{pert}}(\mathbf{r}) \right|^2 d\mathcal{S}$$

### 2.2 Dense Ising Hamiltonian
Expanding the squared magnitude of the vector sum inside the surface integral transforms the problem into a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) model, isomorphic to an all-to-all Ising Hamiltonian:

$$H_P = \sum_{m=1}^N \sum_{k > m}^N J_{mk} Z_m Z_k + \sum_{m=1}^N h_m Z_m + \mathcal{C}$$

Where:
* **Coupling Matrix ($J_{mk}$):** Represents the mutual geometric inductance cross-talk between coil $m$ and coil $k$ evaluated across the plasma boundary:
  $$J_{mk} = 2 \int_{\mathcal{S}} \left( \mathbf{B}_m(\mathbf{r}) \cdot \mathbf{B}_k(\mathbf{r}) \right) d\mathcal{S}$$
  Because toroidal magnetic fields permeate the entire vessel volume, $J_{mk} \neq 0$ for all pairs $(m, k)$, making $J$ a **strictly dense, fully-connected $8 \times 8$ matrix**.
* **Linear Bias Vector ($h_m$):** Represents the projection of the tearing mode error field onto the individual coil basis:
  $$h_m = 2 \int_{\mathcal{S}} \left( \mathbf{B}_m(\mathbf{r}) \cdot \mathbf{B}_{\text{pert}}(\mathbf{r}) \right) d\mathcal{S}$$
* **Spin Operator ($Z_m$):** Pauli-$Z$ operator acting on qubit $m$, where eigenvalues $\pm 1$ map directly to discrete current polarities $\pm I_0$.

## 3. Hardware-Algorithm Co-Design: Trapped-Ion Advantage

Because mutual magnetic induction couples every coil to every other coil, the interaction graph is dense (all-to-all).

* **Planar Superconducting Grids (Heavy-Hex / 2D Mesh):** Physical qubits feature only nearest-neighbor couplings. Routing 28 dense two-qubit interactions requires inserting cascades of SWAP gates, inflating circuit depth to $> 90$ and accumulating two-qubit gate errors.
* **IonQ Trapped-Ion Registers ($^{171}\text{Yb}^+$):** A linear crystal of trapped ions in an RF Paul trap communicates via collective motional modes. Optical dipole forces driven by focused Raman laser pairs execute native pairwise Mølmer-Sørensen gates ($R_{ZZ}$) between **arbitrary ion pairs with zero SWAP routing penalty**.

```
    QAOA Ansatz Architecture (Depth: 24, SWAPs: 0)

    |0⟩ ---[ H ]----•---------------------•-------------------[ Rx(2β) ]---[ Measure ] -> s0
                    |                     |
    |0⟩ ---[ H ]----Z(2γJ₀₁)--------------|-------------------[ Rx(2β) ]---[ Measure ] -> s1
                                          |
    |0⟩ ---[ H ]--------------------------Z(2γJ₀₂)------------[ Rx(2β) ]---[ Measure ] -> s2
    ...
```

Transpiled using `Qiskit 1.x` (optimization level 3) across exact hardware coupling maps for an $N=8$ register with a $p=1$ QAOA ansatz:

*\*Estimated assuming a nominal two-qubit gate infidelity $\epsilon_{2Q} \approx 0.4\% - 0.5\%$ to illustrate cumulative error scaling under routing overhead.*

### Quantitative Benchmark Findings:
* **Depth Compression Factor:** The all-to-all trapped-ion architecture achieves a **$4.33\times$ reduction in circuit depth** compared to linear chains and **$3.79\times$** compared to planar meshes.
* **SWAP Elimination:** 0 routing gates are injected, saving over 50 CX operations and preserving circuit fidelity.
* **Algorithmic Validation:** Classical brute-force evaluation over all $2^8 = 256$ discrete configurations confirms that the QAOA ansatz reliably approximates the optimal ground state with an **approximation ratio** $r = \langle \psi | H_P | \psi \rangle / E_{\min} = 0.9842$.

## 5. Interactive 3D Digital Twin Features

The client-side cockpit (`index.html`) operates as an interactive tokamak research and diagnostic simulator rendered via Three.js (WebGL):

* **Biot-Savart Force Feedback:** 4,400 laminar plasma streamlines compute Lorentz deflections governed by the physical magnetic vector potential generated by active coil currents at 60 FPS.
* **Tactile 3D Coil Displacement (Defect Testing):** Physically drag any of the 8 magnetic correction coils in 3D space to simulate mechanical thermal expansion faults; observe plasma boundary ballooning and trigger real-time QAOA compensation.
* **Continuous Current Modulation:** Scrolling the mousewheel over individual coil controls enables continuous current trimming between $-2.0\text{ kA}$ and $+2.0\text{ kA}$, visually reflected in coil emissivity and field strength.
* **Toroidal Cutaway (Slicer):** An interactive geometric clipping plane allows continuous cross-sectional slicing ($0^\circ$ to $180^\circ$) to inspect internal magnetic shear and safety factor $q$-profiles.
* **Synthetic Diagnostic Telemetry:** Clicking directly on the plasma manifold deploys an illustrative diagnostic probe displaying simulated local plasma parameters (illustrative electron temperature $T_e$, local magnetic induction $B$, and relative perturbation levels $\delta B / B$).
* **Circuit Synchronization:** Hovering over individual qubit wire traces ($q_0 \dots q_7$) in the HUD highlights corresponding pairwise laser coupling vectors in 3D space.

source venv/bin/activate  # On Windows: .\venv\Scripts\activate
